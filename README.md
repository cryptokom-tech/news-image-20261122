The World in One Day — Sunday 22 November 2026: a front-page image summing up 22 November 2026's world news, 36 numbered stories from six continents, with a dotted world map carrying a numbered pin for each story.

The image names its day in a red banner (Sunday 22 November 2026) and
each story is readable on its own: a headline plus a one- or two-line
summary, so a viewer who missed the day can tell what happened from
the image alone.

## The stories

The round 1 morning edition, researched on the morning of
22 November 2026 from Reuters, BBC, Jagran Josh, Newsmriti,
NationPress and other digests — only events that happened, or were
first reported, on 22 November 2026. Leading stories: IG Metall
calls a 24-hour strike for Monday (23,000 workers, 19 firms),
COP31 deadlocked into Sunday over fossil fuels, France and the US
draft a UN resolution for the post-UNIFIL vacuum, US–China military
talks end in Shanghai, the EU proposes budget cuts and a digital
tax, Modi opens the Nitish Setu bridge in Bihar, and the ATP Finals
semis are set.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, verifying the PNG signature, the
IHDR dimensions (1024x1024), the 1 MiB size limit, the IEND chunk,
and that the written bytes match the bundle exactly. It uses no
network, and both runs write identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
